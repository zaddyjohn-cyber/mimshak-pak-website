About Mimshak Pak

A trusted packaging manufacturer in Asaba

Mimshak Pak Investments Ltd. is a registered Nigerian packaging manufacturer based in Asaba, Delta State. We combine our own machinery, an experienced team and quality materials to produce reliable packaging for businesses across different industries.

Printing, forming and finishing all happen on our own floor, and printed nylon is produced through our subsidiary, Mimshak Integrated Packages. That is what lets us hold a specification steady from the first order through every reorder.
