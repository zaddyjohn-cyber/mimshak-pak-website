Carton Printing

Printed cartons made for your product

Mimshak Pak Investments Ltd. provides carton printing in Asaba for businesses that need branded cartons for product packaging, storage, retail display, shipping and industrial use. Carton printing is the process of producing cartons with your design, brand name, product details and artwork printed directly onto durable board.

Good printed cartons do two jobs at once: they protect your product through handling and transport, and they present your brand to the customer. We help Nigerian businesses get both, with clean printing, solid construction and consistent finishing on every order.

Common Questions

Carton printing questions

Do you print cartons for businesses outside Asaba?

Yes. We print and deliver cartons for businesses in Asaba, across Delta State and in other parts of Nigeria.

Can you print my logo and product details on the carton?

Yes. We provide multi-colour printing of your logo, brand colours, product details and any batch or regulatory information you need on the box.

Can I order cartons in a custom size?

Yes. Cartons are produced to your product size, weight and presentation requirements rather than to a fixed catalogue size.

What do you need from me to start?

Your product dimensions, the print colours or artwork, and the quantity you need. If you do not have artwork ready, send what you have and we will work through it with you.
