Common Questions

Before you get in touch

Do you produce cartons for businesses outside Asaba?

Yes. We produce printed cartons and custom carton packaging for businesses in Asaba, across Delta State and in other parts of Nigeria.

Do you print nylon packaging?

Yes. Nylon print packaging is handled through our subsidiary, Mimshak Integrated Packages, on the same site in Asaba.

Can I order custom carton sizes?

Yes. We produce cartons based on your product size, packaging need and quantity.

Can you print my business name or logo?

Yes. We provide multi-colour carton printing and printed nylon for branded product packaging.

What do you need to prepare a quote?

The product you are packaging, approximate dimensions, the format you want (carton or nylon), the print colours and your expected order quantity. If you are unsure of any of these, send what you have and we will work through it with you.

Prefer to get a price first?

Send your specification through the quote form and we will come back with costs and lead time.
