Common Questions

Nylon packaging questions

What is Mimshak Integrated Packages?

It is the nylon print packaging subsidiary of Mimshak Pak Investments Ltd., operating from the same facility in Asaba, Delta State.

Can you print in multiple colours?

Yes. We produce multicolour branded printing on nylon film for retail, food and product packaging.

Can nylon be cut to my product size?

Yes. We make custom cuts on nylon packaging to fit your product rather than supplying only fixed sizes.

Can I order cartons and nylon together?

Yes. Both formats are produced in-house, so they can be quoted and produced together under one specification.
