Common Questions

Custom carton questions

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Order sizes depend on the carton specification. Tell us the quantity you have in mind and we will confirm what is workable for your product.

Can you match a carton I already use?

Yes. Send us a sample or the dimensions and print details of your current carton and we can produce to the same specification.

Can you handle both small retail cartons and heavy shipping cartons?

Yes. Board strength and construction are chosen to suit the product, from light retail packs through to heavy-duty distribution cartons.

Will repeat orders match the first run?

Yes. We hold your specification so reorders come back the same size, strength and print as the original production run.
