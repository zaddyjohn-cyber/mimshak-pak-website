Before You Commit

Why ask for a sample first

Packaging is easier to judge in your hand than on a screen. A sample lets you check it properly before money changes hands.

Check the board strength

Make sure it holds your product's weight and survives the journey to your customer.

See the real print quality

Colours print differently on carton board and nylon film than they look on a screen.

Confirm the size fits

Put your actual product inside it before we run thousands of units.

Then order with confidence

Once the sample is right, we produce the full run to exactly that specification.

Samples of existing stock are free Custom printed samples are chargeable

Note on custom samples: producing a one-off printed sample still needs a printing plate, so custom samples carry a cost. That cost comes off your first bulk order. Samples of packaging we already produce are free — just cover the delivery.
