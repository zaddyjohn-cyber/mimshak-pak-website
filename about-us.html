Who We Are

Carton and nylon packaging, produced under one roof

Mimshak Pak Investments Ltd. is a fully registered Nigerian manufacturing company located in Asaba, Delta State. The company specialises in carton printing and carton packaging production for businesses that need reliable, high-quality packaging for consumer products, domestic use and industrial purposes.

Over the years we have grown by building strong relationships with satisfied customers, through excellent customer service, reliable production and consistent quality. When a business partners with us they get a packaging supplier that takes their product, their brand and their deadlines seriously.

Through our subsidiary, Mimshak Integrated Packages, we also focus on nylon print packaging production, helping businesses create branded nylon packaging for retail, food, household, industrial and product packaging needs.

Our Mission

To manufacture high-quality cartons and nylon packaging materials that help businesses package, protect and present their products professionally.

Our Vision

To become one of Nigeria's trusted names in carton printing, nylon printing and custom packaging production.
